Smart Laptop Shopping

Laptop Buying Guide

Start with your needs, then choose the performance and features that actually matter.

◈

1. Start With Your Use

Gaming, business, study, creative work and everyday browsing all need different levels of performance. Start with the category that matches your routine.

⚡

2. Check Performance

Look at the processor, memory and graphics capability together. More demanding workloads benefit from stronger CPUs, more RAM and dedicated graphics.

▣

3. Choose Enough Storage

SSD storage gives a fast everyday experience. Choose capacity based on your applications, documents, media and games rather than price alone.

▤

4. Think About Display

Consider screen size, resolution, brightness and refresh rate. A larger high-quality display can be valuable for creative work and gaming.

⌁

5. Balance Portability

If you travel or carry your laptop daily, pay attention to weight, dimensions, battery expectations and connectivity.

₹

6. Match Your Budget

Set a realistic budget first and prioritize the features you will use most. Avoid paying extra for specifications that do not improve your actual workflow.
